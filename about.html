About LAstarworks

LAstarworks is a long‑term personal project created by Goran Mandić, known as Limeniks. It connects astronomy, DIY engineering, 3D printing, and artistic installations into a single evolving story — built slowly, intentionally, and with a deep fascination for the night sky.

Located in the quiet rural village of Piljenice, with low light pollution and open horizons, LAstarworks grows as both a technical workshop and a creative space. Every part of the project — from mechanical design to visual storytelling — is built by hand, piece by piece.

Bastion Observatory

One of the central elements of LAstarworks is the Bastion Observatory — a unique 3D‑printed astronomical installation combining engineering, art, and real astrophotography hardware. The Bastion Observatory is designed as a symbolic and functional structure that merges creativity with scientific purpose.

Bastion Telescope

Inside the installation sits the Bastion Telescope, powered by an OpenAstroTracker system — an open‑source GoTo mount capable of precise celestial tracking. This fusion of open hardware and artistic design represents the core philosophy of LAstarworks: science as a creative journey.

The 3D‑Printed Astronaut Helmet

A full‑scale 3D‑printed astronaut helmet forms the outer shell of the Bastion Observatory. Printed in multiple PETG segments and assembled manually, it serves as both a sculpture and a functional housing for astronomical equipment.

A Science‑Art Installation in Piljenice

The Bastion installation will become the central outdoor piece in the LAstarworks garden — a small science‑art environment where technology, nature, and imagination coexist under the stars.

  • a symbolic landmark of exploration,
  • a functional astronomical instrument,
  • a STEM educational piece,
  • and an artistic sculpture illuminated by the night sky.

Open Hardware, Open Sky

LAstarworks embraces open‑source tools, modular engineering, and accessible technology. The project demonstrates that advanced astronomical equipment can be built at home with creativity, patience, and experimentation.
